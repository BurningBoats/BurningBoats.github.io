We burned the boats. There's no way back — so we built a proper home for what's ahead.

## Why a new site

Until now, everything about the studio lived in scattered places: a trailer here, a Discord there, a Steam page somewhere else. This site pulls it together. It's the one place where you can find out what Mindaro is, who is building it, and how to follow along.

## What's here today

- **Mindaro** — what the game is, its pillars, the creatures you'll meet (some of them), the biomes you'll descend through, and the fastest way to help us: [wishlist it on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3879330/).
- **The team** — the nine of us, drawn as the pioneers we send down there.
- **Press kit** — logos, key art, screenshots and a fact sheet, free to use for coverage.
- **Careers** — a few specific areas where we're looking for help.

## What's coming

The trailer, real in-game screenshots, and devlogs as development moves forward. Updates land on Discord first, then here.

## How to follow

Wishlist Mindaro on Steam, join the [Discord](https://discord.gg/bxkRXGFtz7), and follow us on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/burningboatsss/).

See you below.
