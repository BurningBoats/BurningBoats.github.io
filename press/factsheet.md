## Short description

Mindaro is a 1–6 player co-op action-horror game. Descend into an abandoned mining planet, scavenge for what keeps you alive, learn the creatures' patterns and recover what you were sent for — or don't come back.

## Long description

Mindaro is a co-op action-horror game for 1 to 6 players. Descend into a hostile world, slip past alien creatures and survive long enough to uncover the secrets buried in the depths.

A crew of pioneers is sent to recover high-value assets scattered through the depths of Mindaro — an abandoned mining planet crawling with strange life forms and enigmatic anomalies that warp reality in stranger ways the deeper you go.

Mindaro is built around learnable creatures, chaotic co-op and hard choices under pressure. Every biome is a layer with its own ecosystem, light and rules; spatial audio warns you before your eyes do; and every trip down is a trade between what you carry and how long you stay. Play alone or with up to five friends. Whether you split up or stick together, both are a risk.

## Features

- Strange creatures — every creature has its own patterns. Learn them, then decide: face it or run.
- Chaotic horror — nothing is static. Plans fall apart; that's when it gets fun.
- Team dynamics — split up or stick together. Both are a risk.
- Resource management — every trip down is a trade: how much you carry, how long you stay.
- Rich ecosystems — layered biomes, each with its own life and its own rules.
- Spatial audio — sound is your first warning. Sometimes your only one.
- 1–6 player online co-op
- Ten named layers: Archiland, Lush, Base, Fungi, Saturn, Titan Forest, Middle Quarry, Galvanic, Crystal, The Core

## History

Burning Boats Studios is an independent studio formed by Mexican developers across programming, design, art and sound. Mindaro, its first title, was pitched at SuperNova 2025 and has been shown with its GCMX trailer. The studio is small by design and works closely with its community on Discord.
